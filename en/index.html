Who it’s for

Network construction for utility, business and public-sector clients.

Our services are aimed at clients whose investments need an electricity network to be built, extended, or connected.

  • Electricity suppliers and licensed network operators
  • Investors in industrial and commercial property
  • General contractors
  • Property developers
  • Municipalities and other public institutions

In practice

When a new industrial facility needs a grid connection, or an area needs a new electricity supply, we take part in the related network construction and installation work.

Not residential electrical work

Our work is at utility and network scale, which is different from residential electrical work such as rewiring a flat or replacing sockets.

Our process

From planning to operation.

A structured approach from the first site assessment to long-term operational support.
